App Instructions

How to use Blowtech Banking

  • Check Balance: Click the 👁️ icon on the Total Balance card to hide or reveal your funds.
  • Send Money: Click the blue "Send money" button at the top right or in Quick Actions to start a transfer.
  • Request Money: Click the "Request" Quick Action to ask for funds.
  • Insights: Click the "Insights" link in the left sidebar to view your categorized spending and investment graphs.
  • Add Money via Card: Click the "Cards" link in the left sidebar to simulate adding funds from an external debit/credit card.
  • Search Transactions: Use the search bar in the "Recent Activity" section to filter by merchant or category.
